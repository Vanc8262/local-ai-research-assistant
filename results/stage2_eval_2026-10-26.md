## What the numbers mean

- **Hybrid reports know more.** They found every expected fact; local missed NF4, double
  quantization and paged optimizers on QLoRA, because all nine of its sources were blogs.
- **Most remaining flags are the checker being strict, not the report being wrong.** A
  sentence listing several items, each from a different source, is checked one source at a
  time and comes out "partial". A hand review of the AWS hybrid report found it correct and
  complete, with six of its seven flags of this kind.
- **The checker does catch real errors.** In the local QLoRA report it flagged the claim
  that LoRA adapters are quantized (they stay 16-bit).
- **Verification is the slow part:** 2 to 34 minutes per report on Gemma, against about
  2 minutes of research.
- **Memory:** llama-server's host memory grew to 14.4 GB during long verification runs and
  free RAM fell to 267 MB, even with a model reload before each question.
- Gemini's free tier handled all 25 writing calls with no rate-limit fallback.
