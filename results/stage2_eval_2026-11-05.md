## What the numbers mean

- **The gate still fails, by about as much as before.** The verifier fixes did not move the
  pass count.
- **37 of 252 claims were flagged:** 15 uncited, 15 partial and 7 unsupported. At least three
  of the uncited ones are headings or list labels, such as "Cost Optimization (also referred
  to as Cost)", which the checker treated as claims. The other flags have not been reviewed by
  hand, so the share that are real errors is unknown.
- **Single runs are noisy.** In the attempt cut off by the outage, local lora-vs-qlora scored
  100% coverage, 100% accuracy and 25% facts. Here the same question scored 88%, 73% and 100%.
  Comparing one run with another cannot show whether a change helped.
- **The gate does not measure completeness.** That same 25%-facts report passed the gate in
  the cut-off attempt, because the gate checks citations and not expected facts.
- **Memory is a risk.** Free RAM fell to 0.09 GB at its lowest, even with model reloads. All 10
  reports still completed.
- **Time:** the eval took 2 hours 6 minutes. Verification is still the slow part, up to about
  30 minutes for the longest report.

## Next

   and verify again.
2. Stop the checker counting headings and list labels as claims.
3. Write local reports at a lower temperature to reduce the swing between runs.
4. Find what uses the memory during verification.
