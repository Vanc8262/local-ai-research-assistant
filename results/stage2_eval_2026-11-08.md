# Stage 2 research eval, two runs per question (2026-11-08)

The same five public questions as the [Nov 5 eval](stage2_eval_2026-11-05b.md), with four changes:

- every question runs twice;
- the QLoRA, AWS and Self-RAG questions also run in codex mode (local research from the codex papers);
- `verify` asks the decider whether the passages support each claim the model passed, and a pass it doubts drops to partial;
- the gate also needs 75% of the expected facts and allows at most 15% of claims to be removed in repair, down from 25%.

There were 26 reports in 4.6 hours, on the RTX 3060 with 16 GB of RAM. RAM use is in [ram_eval_2026-11-08.md](ram_eval_2026-11-08.md).

A pass shows that the checker accepts what is left of a report, not that every sentence is true.

## What the numbers mean

- **The stricter gate fails real weaknesses.** Each of the 8 failures had a clear cause: either fewer than 75% of
  the expected facts were found, or repair removed more than 15% of the claims. None failed on citations after
  repair.
- **Local QLoRA is the weakest result.** Both runs found only "4-bit" out of the four expected facts. The codex
  runs of the same question found all four. So for this question the web sources are what fall short, not the model.
- **Codex reports know more but cite worse.** They found 100% of the expected facts, but only 74% of their cited
  claims were supported before repair. The AWS codex runs lost about half their claims in repair. This points to
  how the local model writes from long book chunks, not to missing sources. That still needs a hand review.
- **The decider rarely doubts local passes but often doubts hybrid ones.** It overruled 70 passes in all: 0 to 4
  per local report, 2 to 14 per hybrid report and 0 to 2 per codex report. It never failed to answer. Its threshold
  (confidence 0.5) still comes from the 36 decide cases, not from hand-labelled claims.
- **Run-to-run swings remain.** 3 of 5 questions passed in both runs in local and in hybrid mode. Two runs
  show the noise, but they are too few to average it out.
- **Verification is still the slow part.** Hybrid reports took a median of 13 minutes to verify, and GGUF took up
  to 29.
