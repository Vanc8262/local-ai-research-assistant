## research / bus-lanes

**Question:** What were the measured effects of the Harlow Valley bus lane pilot, and what concerns were raised?

**Answer:**

The pilot converted 6.4 km of Route 12 to bus-only lanes in March 2024 [1]. Average weekday bus travel time on the corridor fell from 31 minutes to 24 minutes [1]. Car travel times on the parallel road were unchanged within measurement error [1]. A survey of 1,180 riders found that 68% rated reliability as improved [2], and ridership rose 11% in the six months after launch compared with the same months a year earlier [2]. However, nine of 40 surveyed shops on the corridor reported lower foot traffic since the lanes opened [3]. The association asked the city to add 15-minute loading bays on each block [3].

## research / sodium-cells

**Question:** How do the Tessaly sodium-ion cells compare with the lithium iron phosphate (LFP) reference on durability, cost and performance?

**Answer:**

According to the Tessaly Labs cycling study, sodium-ion cells retained 91% capacity after 2,000 cycles at 25 °C, while LFP reference cells retained 93% [1]. Independent cost analysis estimates the sodium-ion pack at $62 per kWh against $89 per kWh for the LFP reference at projected volumes [2]. Regarding performance, measured energy density was 148 Wh/kg, about 20% below the LFP reference, and cells delivered 84% of rated capacity at -20 °C [3]. Overall, the sodium-ion technology offers lower energy density but provides significant cost savings and comparable cycling stability [1][2][3].

Automatic check: pass

## research / hybrid-work

**Question:** What happened at Corvin Analytics after it moved to a hybrid schedule?

**Answer:**

Corvin Analytics moved to a hybrid schedule with three remote days per week in January 2025 [1]. Following this change, voluntary attrition fell from 14% to 9% year over year [1]. Engineering metrics also shifted, with tickets closed per engineer rising 6% [2]. However, median code review turnaround lengthened from 5 hours to 8 hours [2]. Employee sentiment data from 410 respondents indicated that 72% preferred the hybrid schedule [3]. Despite overall preference, 44% of employees with under one year of tenure reported feeling less connected to their team, compared with 21% of longer-tenured staff [3].

## research / lake-orrin

**Question:** Why does Lake Orrin have an algae problem, and what is being done about it?

**Answer:**

Lake Orrin recorded algal blooms on 37 days with phosphorus concentrations averaging 48 µg/L, which exceeded the 30 µg/L target [1]. The study attributes roughly 60% of the lake's phosphorus load to agricultural runoff from the Pell Creek sub-basin [2]. The county council approved $2.3 million for buffer strips along 14 km of Pell Creek [3]. Staff expect the project to cut the phosphorus load by 25% within five years [3].

## research / lattice-pruning

**Question:** How well does lattice pruning work on the Brel-7 model, and what caveats have been reported?

**Answer:**

Lattice pruning removes 40% of the weights of the Brel-7 model [1], with a 0.8 point accuracy loss on the Mirel benchmark [1]. However, an independent replication found a 1.9 point accuracy loss when the calibration set was reduced from 5,000 to 500 examples [2]. Efficiency gains were also observed, with inference latency dropping from 38 ms to 27 ms per request after pruning [3]. Memory usage also fell by 35% [3].
